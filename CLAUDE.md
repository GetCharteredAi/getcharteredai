## Content Standards

### 12-Module Programme Description (canonical)
"The 12-module programme begins with an introduction module, followed by 10 content modules that collectively cover all 11 mandatory RICS competencies, and concludes with a dedicated testing and mock interview module. Monthly subscribers unlock one module per month, paced to the training timeline. Annual subscribers get immediate access to all 12 modules from day one."
