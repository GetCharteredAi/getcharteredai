Before you begin

You will type your answers in this mock. Write each response as you would say it aloud in an APC interview, aiming for around 2–3 minutes per question.

This mock lets you practise:

  1. What you would say.
  2. How you structure your answer.
  3. The examples and RICS references you use.
  4. Whether your reasoning is clear.
  5. How much you can cover in two to three minutes.
This is a private practice environment. It will help you identify what to strengthen, but it does not predict your assessment outcome or replace advice from your counsellor or supervisor.
