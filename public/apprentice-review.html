What this is not

This is a professional-development diagnostic, not an EPA or apprenticeship assessment

It does not certify competence, assess against ST0331, or predict EPA outcomes. What it gives you is structured evidence of your professional development progress — and a clear map of what to focus on next.

The apprentices who develop most effectively are the ones who understand where their gaps sit and address them deliberately. This review is the starting point for that.
