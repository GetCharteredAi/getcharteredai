One product. Two development points.

Entering a professional role. Progressing within it.

Professional Readiness supports two different moments in development — one focused on the transition into professional work, the other on progression as experience and responsibility grow.

Part One · After Onboarding

Professional Readiness Benchmark

Typically 8–12 weeks after joining, once there has been enough workplace experience to reflect on.

A clearer picture of how someone is settling into their professional role.

Employee reflection
How they feel they are developing and where they need support.
Manager perspective
Observed strengths, development needs and workplace opportunities.
Michael brings the perspectives together
Identifies development priorities and suggests useful actions and conversation prompts.
The Outcome
A more focused development conversation and agreed actions.

Employee reflections remain private. Managers receive development outcomes and conversation prompts — not raw answers.

Part Two · As Capability Develops

Professional Readiness Review

A deeper, employee-owned review of developing technical and professional capability.

Understanding how knowledge and workplace experience are translating into professional capability.

Technical knowledge
What do they understand within their pathway?
Application and judgement
Can they apply that knowledge, explain their reasoning and recognise their limits?
Progression
What is developing well, and what knowledge or experience should they focus on?
The Outcome
A more informed technical and professional development conversation.

Part Two can be used at appropriate development stages and does not depend on completing Part One.
