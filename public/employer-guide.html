Professional Readiness

Structured support that starts at the beginning

Professional Readiness is the foundation of the Get Chartered employer offer — a structured diagnostic and review process designed to support developing professionals and give managers meaningful, actionable insight.

We don't just identify where someone needs development — we help identify why, so the next action is more useful.

🔍
Part One

Professional Readiness Benchmark

A structured 30-question assessment covering five professional readiness dimensions. Candidates complete the benchmark independently — typically around 60 minutes. Michael then synthesises the responses and produces a personalised readiness report.

  • Five assessment areas: understanding, application, evidencing, professional judgement, and self-awareness
  • Personalised readiness report identifying development priorities
  • Distinguishes learning gaps from exposure gaps from articulation gaps
  • Available from early in the training period — no prior study required
💬
Part Two

Professional Readiness Review

The employer-facilitated half. A structured conversation between a manager or counsellor and the candidate, informed by the Part One report. Michael prepares the agenda: specific questions, areas to probe, and suggested development actions.

  • Manager receives a structured conversation guide — not a generic report
  • Questions are tailored to the individual's benchmark results
  • Employee privacy respected — managers see priorities, not raw answers
  • Designed to make manager time count, not replace it
🗣️
Manager conversations

Better-prepared development conversations

The Professional Readiness Review gives managers a structured entry point into a development conversation that might otherwise be hard to start. The conversation guide provides specific, relevant prompts — not generic questions.

  • Conversation prompts tailored to the individual candidate
  • Covers both technical gaps and professional readiness dimensions
  • Supports managers who are supervising outside their own pathway specialism
  • Reduces the burden on experienced staff to design development conversations from scratch
📊
Workforce Intelligence

Visibility across your developing cohort

Where employers have multiple developing professionals, Workforce Intelligence provides aggregated insight — common development themes, capability gaps, and areas where structured support would have the widest impact.

  • Anonymised aggregate view — individual privacy preserved
  • Common development patterns identified across your cohort
  • Supports L&D prioritisation and programme planning
  • More consistent visibility than one-to-one counsellor impression

AI prepares the conversation; it does not replace it.

Supporting the route to chartership

Most APC referrals aren't caused by lack of knowledge

They happen because technically capable candidates cannot demonstrate what they know in the format assessors require. Counsellor support is valuable — but it rarely addresses this specific gap.

Get Chartered sits alongside existing graduate schemes, apprenticeship providers, counsellors and internal technical training — not instead of them.

📋

Evidence gaps, not knowledge gaps

Candidates describe their work rather than evidence professional judgement. Get Chartered teaches the specific structure assessors score.

🎯

No structured mock interview practice

Most candidates arrive at final assessment having never answered a probing follow-up question under pressure. The 60-minute AI simulator changes that.

⏰

Counsellors can't be available 24/7

Michael is there when your counsellor isn't — 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

Why it matters to employers

The case for structured development support

Investing in structured professional development is not just good for candidates — it creates more efficient use of management time and more consistent team capability.

🎯

Clearer development priorities

Professional Readiness identifies whether a gap is about learning, practice, articulation or workplace exposure — so the next step is targeted, not generic.

💬

Better-prepared manager conversations

Managers receive a structured conversation guide tailored to the individual — not a generic set of questions. Less time preparing, more time where experience matters.

🔍

Learning gap vs. exposure gap

Get Chartered helps managers distinguish between someone who hasn't been taught something and someone who hasn't had the opportunity to apply it yet.

📊

Consistent visibility across teams

Workforce Intelligence surfaces common development themes across your cohort — replacing the impression a line manager has with something more reliable and comparable.

⏱️

Better use of management time

Experienced managers are most valuable when they are sharing judgement and opening doors — not teaching the basics. Get Chartered prepares the foundations so those conversations can happen.

🏗️

Repeatable development foundations

Rather than relying on individual managers to reinvent development support from scratch, Get Chartered provides a structured approach that works consistently across your whole cohort.

Get Chartered helps build repeatable foundations and prepare better development conversations — so manager time is focused where experience matters most.
