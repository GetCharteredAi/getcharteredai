How it works

Five steps from benchmark to insight.

A straightforward process that requires minimal employer administration. Participants and managers engage at their own pace.

1
Participant completes the Benchmark

Each graduate or apprentice completes the 15-question Professional Readiness Benchmark independently. Takes approximately 15 minutes.

2
Manager adds their perspective

The participant’s manager or counsellor completes a short structured reflection on the same five areas. A separate, independent view.

3
Michael identifies patterns

Michael analyses responses across your group and identifies where development is progressing, where support may help and where priorities align or differ.

4
Employer sees the group overview

You receive access to the Professional Readiness Overview — a group-level dashboard showing development patterns across your graduates and apprentices.

5
Review progress later

Run the Progress Reflection to see what has changed. Compare where the group was at Benchmark against where they are now.

What the employer sees

Six views. One clear picture.

The Professional Readiness Overview gives you a structured group-level view across six dimensions of professional development.

01
Where your graduates and apprentices are developing well

Clear signal on where professional practice is progressing across the group — the areas where development is taking hold.

02
Where support may help

Areas where the group data suggests structured support or learning would make a meaningful difference to professional development.

03
Where workplace exposure may be limited

Where participants are reporting limited opportunity to practise key professional behaviours — an experience signal, not a capability judgement.

04
Where priorities align — or differ

How candidate, manager and Michael perspectives point across the five areas. Alignment suggests shared recognition; difference often shows where a conversation would be useful.

05
What support appears to be needed

The pattern of development needs across the group, classified by type — so the right kind of support can be identified and considered.

06
What to consider next

Specific, evidence-based conditions you can create to support professional development. Derived from structured group evidence, not generic recommendations.
