The employer challenge

Training completed is not the same as capability developed.

Once people begin doing real work, employers need to understand more than what courses they have attended or what knowledge they have been taught. They need to know whether the individual can:

  • apply what they know
  • explain their reasoning
  • exercise professional judgement
  • recognise when to ask or escalate
  • take increasing responsibility
  • demonstrate improvement through real workplace activity

Get Chartered AI is designed to work alongside university, apprenticeship providers, employer training and RICS learning — not to replace them. It provides the practice and evidence layer that connects formal learning to workplace performance.

For managers

Focus your judgement where it matters most.

Get Chartered AI structures the evidence and conversation before a development discussion begins — so managers arrive with a clearer picture and can spend their time on the judgements that only an experienced person can make.

A clearer picture before the development conversation

Professional Readiness brings together the individual’s own view and the manager’s observed perspective on the same development areas, before the conversation begins. Both sides arrive with shared, structured context rather than starting from scratch.

Strengths, development priorities and conversation prompts

Managers receive relevant individual development outcomes and structured conversation starters — not raw private answers. The employee’s detailed reasoning remains theirs.

Earlier visibility of where development is needed

Identify gaps in knowledge, application or exposure before they affect performance or assessment outcomes — rather than discovering them close to a submission or assessment date.

A later review that shows whether things have changed

The same approach used again later tells you whether development has moved. The review is not a one-time diagnostic — it is one part of a connected development loop.

Greater visibility of the type of gap

Whether the issue is knowledge, practice, experience, exposure, evidence-recognition or articulation makes a real difference to what support is needed. The system helps surface that distinction before the conversation.

Structured APC support without replacing counsellors

Get Chartered AI sits alongside the people already supporting development. Candidates arrive at counsellor conversations better prepared and with clearer questions.

Employee privacy. Employees keep their detailed responses private. Managers receive development outcomes and useful prompts, not a transcript of private reasoning.
For the wider organisation

A clearer picture across teams and intakes.

When the same structured approach is used consistently, individual development signals begin to show patterns that are harder to see from one conversation at a time.

When the same structured approach is used consistently across teams and early-career groups, individual development signals can be examined collectively — making it easier to see common strengths, recurring development gaps, experience or exposure shortages, capability priorities and differences between cohorts, teams or disciplines.

How support develops over a career

Starters. Minders. Leaders.

Get Chartered AI can support people progressively — not only at one assessment point.

Starters
Learn the work.
Early-career · Apprentices · Graduates

Build knowledge, confidence and professional habits. Begin to understand how to apply what you know, when to ask and how to explain your reasoning.

Minders
Take responsibility for the work.
Developing professionals · APC candidates

Strengthen judgement, accountability, client communication and independent decision-making. Demonstrate capability through what you can actually do, not only through what you have attended.

Leaders
Lead the work.
Experienced professionals · Post-chartership

Develop the capability to lead people, decisions, clients and increasingly complex work. Take on greater professional responsibility and accountability.
