One connected development system

Identify → Practise & Apply → Evidence → Review

One connected system — each stage builds on the last, from identifying what needs to develop through to reviewing whether capability has actually changed.

Identify

Professional Readiness Review

A structured readiness review helps the individual and manager understand strengths, development needs and where more experience, exposure or confidence may be required.

Practise & Apply

Michael

Michael gives the individual a safe place to apply knowledge, explain reasoning, respond to challenge and strengthen professional judgement before taking that development back into the workplace.

Evidence

Workplace demonstration

Development activity is connected to real workplace experience so improvement is demonstrated through what the individual can actually do — not simply through training attendance.

Review

Later readiness check

Return later and ask the important question: has anything actually changed? The review shows whether capability has developed, where further support is needed and what the next priority should be.

Professional judgement remains human-led throughout. Get Chartered AI structures the evidence and conversation — it does not replace the manager, the counsellor or the individual’s own development.

How support develops over a career

Starters. Minders. Leaders.

Get Chartered AI can support people progressively — not only at one assessment point.

Starters
Learn the work.
Early-career · Apprentices · Graduates

Build knowledge, confidence and professional habits. Begin to understand how to apply what you know, when to ask and how to explain your reasoning.

Minders
Take responsibility for the work.
Developing professionals · APC candidates

Strengthen judgement, accountability, client communication and independent decision-making. Demonstrate capability through what you can actually do, not only through what you have attended.

Leaders
Lead the work.
Experienced professionals · Post-chartership

Develop the capability to lead people, decisions, clients and increasingly complex work. Take on greater professional responsibility and accountability.
