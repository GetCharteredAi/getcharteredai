A closer look at Professional Readiness

What happens in the first few months after onboarding?

Professional Readiness supports that early transition — once onboarding is complete and real workplace development begins.

Once real work begins, developing professionals are no longer just learning tasks and systems. They are learning when to ask, when to act, how to explain their reasoning and how to exercise judgement — and that does not happen automatically.

Part One

Professional Readiness Benchmark

An early readiness review that brings together the employee’s own view and the manager’s observed perspective on the same development areas.

Part Two

Professional Readiness Review

Pathway-specific technical questions and professional judgement scenarios — showing whether learning and workplace experience are translating into professional capability.

Workforce Intelligence

A consistent view across your team

When the same structured approach is used consistently, individual signals build a picture of shared strengths, recurring gaps and priority development areas.

The employer journey

How support develops over time

Different stages need different kinds of support — from the first months after onboarding through deeper technical development and on to chartership.

1
Entering a professional role
Part One — Professional Readiness Benchmark
Typically 8–12 weeks after joining, once onboarding is complete

Helps the employee and manager understand how the person is beginning to operate professionally and what should happen next.

2
Building technical capability
First technical pathway review
Around end of Year One, into Year Two, or at annual-review stage

A structured pathway-specific technical check of developing knowledge, application and judgement.

3
Deepening technical capability
Advanced technical pathway benchmark
Further into development — typically Year Two or Three, depending on the individual

A more demanding technical review of depth, application and professional judgement as knowledge and responsibility grow.

4
Preparing for chartership
12 Module APC Programme

Structured preparation across competencies, pathway-specific technical knowledge and assessment readiness for candidates who need the full APC route.

5
Approaching assessment
Final Sprint

For candidates close to chartership assessment — check readiness, identify weaker areas, sharpen answers and practise under pressure.
