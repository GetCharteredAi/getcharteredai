RICS Case Study Requirements

Your case study is strictly limited to 3,000 words maximum. This includes headings and subheadings but excludes the cover page, contents page and appendices.

The recommended structure is:

  • Introduction — approximately 400 words
  • Approach — approximately 1,000 words
  • Achievements — approximately 1,200 words
  • Conclusion — approximately 400 words

Source: RICS APC submissions and assessment guidance
