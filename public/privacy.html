Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you use Get Chartered AI.

1. Who We Are

Get Chartered AI is an online APC preparation platform available at getcharteredai.com. We are the data controller for the personal data you provide to us. Contact us at info@getcharteredai.com with any privacy queries.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process your subscription payment via Stripe
  • Send you a welcome email and important account communications
  • Provide access to course content
  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Improve the platform and fix issues

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for unsolicited marketing without your consent.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under UK GDPR:

  • Contract — to provide you with the service you have purchased
  • Legitimate interests — to improve our platform and prevent fraud
  • Legal obligation — where required by law

5. Third Parties

We share data with the following trusted third parties only where necessary:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe processes your card details directly and securely. We never see or store your card number. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Netlify — platform hosting and serverless functions. See Netlify's Privacy Policy.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (welcome emails). See Resend's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic — Michael and Mock Simulator in Module 12. Queries you submit to the AI tutor are processed by Anthropic's API. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

Our platform uses browser local storage (not traditional cookies) to:

  • Keep you logged in between sessions
  • Save your course progress
  • Remember your plan type

This data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers except when verifying your login. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser storage or logging out.

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies or analytics cookies at this time.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your personal data
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@getcharteredai.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure token-based authentication, and restricted access to data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure but we take our obligations seriously.

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards in case of disputes or legal obligations. When data is no longer needed we delete it securely.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

11. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at info@getcharteredai.com.
