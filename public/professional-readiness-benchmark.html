How your responses are used

Privacy notice

Your reflection is a private conversation with Michael. Your specific answers, reasoning and examples are for your development report only.

Your manager will receive a development summary — themes, conversation prompts and suggested priorities — without seeing your individual responses.

Your employer can see that you have completed the Benchmark and your overall progress status. They cannot see your individual responses or development report.

There is no pass or fail outcome. The Benchmark is a development tool, not a performance assessment.
