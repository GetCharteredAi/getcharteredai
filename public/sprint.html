03 — Strengthen

Recognise when an answer is not enough.

One of the most important preparation skills is being able to identify an answer that falls short — before an assessor does. Sprint helps you spot answers that are:

Too descriptive — telling rather than demonstrating
Incomplete — lacking application or evidence
Poorly structured — no clear reasoning thread
Not at the right level — knowledge only, when judgement is required
Unsupported — no reasoning or experience behind the answer
Missing the question — not addressing the competency being tested
Don't just find the wrong answer. Learn why it is insufficient and how to strengthen it.
