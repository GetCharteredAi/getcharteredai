What this is not

This is a professional-development diagnostic, not an RICS assessment

It does not certify competence or guarantee APC success. What it gives you is structured evidence of progress towards professional and assessment readiness — and a clear map of what to do next.

The candidates who pass comfortably are not always the most experienced. They are the ones who understood what was expected, knew where their gaps sat, and addressed them deliberately. This review is the starting point for that.
